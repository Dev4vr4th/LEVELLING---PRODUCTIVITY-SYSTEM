## ◆ How It Works

1. **Set your Role**  
   General for a full training quest with running, or Student to swap running for a Study Session.

2. **Clear your Daily Quest**  
   The System generates a quest every day, tuned to your level. Miss a day and the Penalty System comes for you.

3. **Defeat bosses, rank up**  
   Hit a boss’s stat and level requirements, land the finishing blow in real life, and watch your Hunter Rank climb — E to S, over roughly a year of real consistency.

## Onboarding

- One-time setup quiz: codename, height, weight, age, available equipment, baseline push-up/squat ability, cardio level, and training goal (strength / endurance / balanced / fat loss).
- Generates your starting stats and calibrates your first Daily Quest difficulty.
- Ends with an **“Awakening”** cutscene before you enter the app.

---

## Hunter Rank System

- **Six ranks**: `E → D → C → B → A → S`, each tied to a level threshold.
- Leveling up alone can raise you *up to* the rank where the next Boss lives — but **defeating that Boss** is what actually unlocks entry into the next rank. You can’t level-grind past a Boss you haven’t beaten.
- Rank is shown on a glowing badge at the top of the app, with its own color per rank.
- The whole interface tints toward your current rank’s color as you climb (blue → violet → crimson → gold).

---

## Stats

- Four RPG stats: **Strength**, **Agility**, **Vitality**, **Sense**.
- Earned automatically from clearing your Daily Quest, logging workouts, and custom routines.
- Each stat has a cap (60 by default) — raised permanently by certain Store accessories, but the stat itself always has to be earned through real activity, never bought directly.

---

## Daily Quest

- One mandatory quest per day: push-ups, sit-ups, squats, and a run (or Study Session), scaled to your level.
- Targets grow gently as you level up, but are capped at sane numbers (max ~24–30 reps, ~3 km run at the highest levels) — never anything like “100 push-ups.”
- **Rest day** once a week (no quest, streak stays safe) and a **light/recovery day** (targets halved) — built-in recovery instead of daily max intensity.
- Clearing it awards XP and gold, and contributes to your streak.

---

## The Penalty System

Miss a day (without a Streak Shield active) and the System responds for real:

- Your rank **visibly demotes** one tier (with its own dramatic red “Rank Down” screen).
- You lose XP and 15% of your gold.
- Your streak resets to 0.

To regain access, you must clear a **Penalty Zone** task (3 sets of 8 burpees).  
The **Monarch’s Cloak** (Store item) forgives **one** missed day per week — a safety net, not a free pass.

## Boss Arena

- Six Bosses, one per rank, based on Solo Leveling antagonists:
  - **Trial Goblin** (E)
  - **Stone Golem** (D)
  - **Igris** (C)
  - **Ice Elf Lord** (B)
  - **Beru the Ant King** (A)
  - **The Architect** (S, final boss)

- Each Boss is **locked** behind real requirements: minimum level, minimum stats, and sometimes a specific achievement.
- Once unlocked, defeating a Boss means completing its real-life **“Finishing Blow”** quest — a mix of fitness and productivity tasks (e.g. push-ups, a run, reading pages, a focus session). Tasks auto-complete if you log matching activity elsewhere in the app while a fight is active.
- Defeating a Boss grants a one-time title (e.g. “Goblin Slayer,” “Shadow Monarch”), a modest XP/gold bonus, a small permanent stat boost, and unlocks the next rank.
- No penalty for not being ready — locked Bosses just show what you’re missing.

---

## Daily XP Cap

- All XP — from quests, custom logging, focus sessions, and routines — is capped at **200 XP per calendar day**.
- This is the actual pacing mechanism: it’s mathematically what keeps the journey to S-rank around a year for a consistent user, and makes it impossible to shortcut by over-logging in one sitting.
- A small readout on the Home tab shows how much of today’s cap you’ve used.

---

## Train Tab

- **Strength Benchmarks**: log your Bench Press, Back Squat, Deadlift, and max Pull-ups, and see your estimated percentile vs. general population norms (illustrative, not clinical).
- **Custom Session Logger**: log push-ups, sit-ups, squats, pull-ups, running, reading, bench, deadlift, or a generic custom strength/cardio session for XP and gold (rate-limited to once per activity type per day for stat gains).
- **Custom Routine Builder**: name your own workout (e.g. “Push Day”), add exercises with sets/reps, save it, and log it as completed whenever you actually do it.

---

## Focus Timer

- A Pomodoro-style timer separate from workouts — for studying or any focused work.
- Presets (25 / 45 / 60 min) or a custom duration; pause and resume.
- Completing a session earns a small amount of XP and gold, and counts toward Boss “focus session” requirements.
- Daily log shows each session and your total focused minutes for the day.

---

## System Store

Spend gold on gear that gives **real passive bonuses** while equipped, not just cosmetic skins:

- **Weapons** → bonus XP (flat or %) from all activity.
- **Armor** → bonus gold from quests, or the weekly Streak Shield.
- **Accessories** (Mana Orb, Iron Will Band, Swiftstep Boots, Shadow Monarch Crown) → permanently raise specific stat ceilings.

All gear bonuses show as small glowing chips under your rank plate when equipped.

## Profile

- Full summary: codename, height/weight, equipment, rank, level, quests cleared, longest streak, achievements unlocked, Bosses defeated.
- **Export Backup**: download your save as a JSON file.
- **Import Backup**: restore a previously exported save (e.g. when moving to a new device or browser).
- **Reset System**: permanently wipes all local data.

---

## Data & Privacy

- Everything lives in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, no account required, no subscription.
- Because it’s local-only, moving between devices requires manually exporting and importing a backup file (Profile tab) — there’s no live cross-device sync.
